# 2026-10-15: Load testing, and the backup and restore drill

## 10. Load test
- **`npm run load-test`:** starts the real server on a real (embedded) Postgres and drives it over HTTP. Or point it at a deployment with `LOAD_TARGET_URL` and `LOAD_API_KEY`.
- **Four scenarios at concurrency 1, 8 and 32:** authenticated reads, full purchase flows (4 writes), Verify API checks, and public tree heads.
- **Baseline** (`docs/perf/load-test-2026-10-15.md`), with zero errors:
  - About 170 purchase flows per second, 60–70 checks per second, and 1,500–2,000 reads per second.
  - Roughly 250× headroom over a 100× burst at the plan's $1M ARR volume.
- **First bottleneck:** the global ledger lock (purchases plateau at 8 concurrent clients). Revisit near 100 sustained writes per second.
