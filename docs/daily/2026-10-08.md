# 2026-10-08: Part 7, stablecoin rail

## Shipped
- **Contract `contracts/src/ProofDeskJobs.sol`** (Foundry, Solidity 0.8.30). It implements the ERC-8183 core interface (`createJob`, `setProvider`, `setBudget`, `fund`, `submit`, `complete`, `reject`, `claimRefund`, `getJob`) for one stablecoin (USDC).
  - Proof Desk is only the **evaluator** address and never holds funds. The most it can do is settle a funded job between that job's own provider and client.
  - Extensions:
    - `createAndFund`: the client posts the agreed price and funds in one transaction.
    - `createAndFundWithAuthorization`: the same, but gasless via EIP-3009 `receiveWithAuthorization`. The nonce is derived from the job terms, so a signature funds exactly one job and can't be redirected or replayed.
    - `settle(jobId, releaseBP, reason)`: release, refund or split, from Funded or Submitted, so sellers never send a transaction.
  - Rules:
    - The fee is capped at 5% and locked into each job when it's created.
    - The provider can't be paid after expiry.
    - `claimRefund` after expiry works for anyone and calls no hooks (hooks are unsupported), so it can't be blocked.
    - Fee-on-transfer tokens are refused.
  - **Tests:** 20 unit and fuzz tests, plus 4 invariants (256 runs × 64 calls, every party including an attacker):
    1. Funds are conserved: the contract holds exactly the live budgets, and nothing is created or lost.
    2. Only the evaluator ever decides a funded job.
    3. The provider is never paid after expiry.
    4. Every expired funded job can be refunded in full, by anyone, at any time.
  - A planted bug (the evaluator check removed) is caught by invariant 2.
- **`packages/chain`:**
  - `ViemChainGateway` (viem 2.57): reads job state; finds the job a funding transaction created; relays gasless funding; settles. Reverts become non-retryable `ChainError`s, and RPC trouble stays retryable.
  - `FakeChainGateway` mirrors the contract's rules, including real EIP-712 signature checks.
  - The ABI and bytecode are generated from the forge build (`npm run contracts:build`; CI checks they're current).
  - `anvil.test.ts` deploys the real contract to a local chain and runs wallet funding, gasless funding, a partial settle with exact balances, revert mapping, and a check that the TS nonce equals the contract's.
- **API:**
  - `PUT/GET /v1/sellers/:ref/wallet`: the seller's USDC payout address.
  - `POST /v1/agreements/:id/onchain-job`: issues fixed terms.
    - provider = seller wallet; evaluator = Proof Desk.
    - description = `proofdesk:v1:<agreement>:<spec hash>`.
    - expiry = delivery deadline + 1 day + appeal window + 7 days.
    - Returns the two wallet calls, plus EIP-712 typed data when `client` is given.
  - `POST …/onchain-job/confirm {tx_hash}`: reads the job back from the chain and requires every term to match (provider, evaluator, description, budget, expiry, contract). A unique index stops one job from backing two agreements.
  - `POST …/onchain-job/authorization`: relays the buyer's signature. Proof Desk pays the gas.
  - Funding after the delivery deadline is refunded on-chain immediately, the same way late card holds are cancelled.
  - **Settlement:** `settle` is sent with the decision's **ledger entry hash as the on-chain `reason`**, so each on-chain outcome points back to the audit trail. Retries are safe: a job that already ended consistently is just recorded.
  - **Scheduler:**
    - Settles early (forced) when a decided job is within 24 h of expiry.
    - Warns ops (`onchain_expiry_near`) when an undecided one is.
    - Records jobs the buyer reclaimed (`onchain_expired`); only a refund decision can settle those.
  - **Payment links with `rail: "onchain"`:** a hosted USDC page (EIP-1193 wallet, one gasless signature; manual calls as a fallback) and **x402** at `/pay/:token/x402`. The x402 flow: 402 with requirements, then `X-PAYMENT` with the signed authorization, then relayed, then 200 with `X-PAYMENT-RESPONSE`. The scheme is `erc8183-job`: a `ReceiveWithAuthorization` to the job contract with the terms-bound nonce. Standard x402 "exact" clients sign `TransferWithAuthorization` and would need this scheme added.
  - Card and on-chain funding are exclusive per agreement.
- **SDK:** `sellers.setWallet/retrieveWallet`, `agreements.createOnchainJob/confirmOnchainFunding/authorizeOnchainFunding/retrieveOnchainJob/createPaymentLink/createReportLink`.
- **MCP:** `fund_purchase_with_usdc` and `submit_usdc_authorization`.
- **`npm run chain:e2e`:** the whole API flow against a real chain. Run on local anvil: funded gaslessly; partial 60% settled; seller received exactly 588,000 (0.588 USDC = 60% − 2% fee); buyer's net cost 600,000; ledger intact.
- **CI:** checks out submodules, installs Foundry, runs `forge test` (ci profile: 512 × 128) and the ABI freshness check.
