# Outreach messages

**How to use:** pick the template for the target's segment, fill in the brackets, and send by email or LinkedIn. Keep it under 120 words. Send 15 in week 1, then follow up once after 4–5 days. Log every send in [tracker.csv](tracker.csv).

Don't call it "escrow" (California licensing rules restrict escrow wording; see [lawyer-brief.md](lawyer-brief.md)). Say "conditional payment", "payment hold" or "pay on proof".

## Translation and localization platforms

> **Subject:** Paying translators only when the translation checks out
>
> Hi [Name], I'm building Proof Desk, which holds payment for a job and releases it only after the deliverable passes checks both sides agreed up front. For translation it automatically catches changed numbers, dates and names, dropped clauses and meaning flips, and sends anything uncertain to a human.
>
> I'd love 30 minutes to learn how [Company] handles quality disputes and rework today. No pitch, just questions, and I'll share what we've measured on 300 test translations.
>
> Would [day] or [day] work?
>
> [Your name]

> [Your name]
